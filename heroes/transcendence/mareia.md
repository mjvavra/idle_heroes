# Purifying Tide - Mareia

## Summary
A Transcendence Mage who hits many enemies at once. Her basic attack applies **Stain**, which lowers the target's damage and can **Freeze** them. Her active skill deals heavy area damage and extends every control effect already on the enemy team.

> Game rule: you can't put more than one Transcendence hero with the same name in a lineup.

## Skills
Skill text copied from the game. The tier at which each skill unlocks is in brackets.

### Active: Phantom Sea Canvases
Deals (1000% of Attack) damage to all enemies, then releases **Chromatic Overflow** 6 times.

- **Chromatic Overflow:** Each deals (1000% of Attack) damage to 1 random enemy and (1000% of Attack) damage to the adjacent heroes of the hit target. If the adjacent target has **Stain**, additionally deals damage equal to 10% of the target's Max. HP to that target (caps at 5000% of the attacker's Attack). This can trigger only once per target per round; afterward, increases the duration of all **Control Effects** carried by all enemies by 1 round.

### Passive: Rippling Chromatics (Tier 2 unlock)
Changes the basic attack to: Attacks 3 times, each attack deals (600% of Attack) damage to 3 random enemies and reduces the target's Control Immunity by 5% for 2 rounds. Inflicts **Stain** on targets that have been attacked 2 or more times for 4 rounds, with a 60% chance to **Freeze** them for 2 rounds. **Stain** will be converted to **Stain · Flash** for Bosses that cannot be controlled.

- **Stain:** Skill effect. Not stackable. Reduces the owner's All Damage Dealt by 10%, and the effect expires after releasing 2 basic attacks or active skills.
- **Stain · Flash:** Skill effect. Not stackable. Reduces the owner's All Damage Dealt by 15%, and the effect expires after releasing 2 basic attacks or active skills.
- **Freeze:** Control effect. Frozen heroes are unable to release their active skills, basic attacks, extra attacks, or counterattacks.

### Passive: Purifying Tidal Waves (Tier 4 unlock)
When **Stain** expires on an enemy on the battlefield, deals (600% of Attack) damage to all enemies, with a 45% chance to grant self 1 layer of **Divine Inspiration Canvases** lasting until the end of the battle.

- **Divine Inspiration Canvases:** Skill effect. Stackable up to 6 layers. Every 2 layers of Divine Inspiration Canvases additionally increases the number of times the active skill releases **Chromatic Overflow** by 1.

### Passive: Ephemeral Canvas Realm (Tier 6 unlock)
At the end of each round, for every 1 enemy with **Stain** on the battlefield, increases self All Damage Dealt by 3% for 1 round, and grants self 4 Energy.

## How she works (analysis, unverified in play)
- **Stain is her engine.** Her basic attack hits 3 times, so enemies hit twice get Stained. That cuts their damage by 10%, gives a 60% chance to Freeze them, and powers her Tier 4 and Tier 6 skills.
- **Her active skill extends control.** Once per target per round, a Chromatic Overflow that hits a Stained enemy adds 1 round to every control effect on the enemy team. That works well with other heroes who stun, freeze or otherwise lock enemies down.
- **She scales during long fights.** Each expiring Stain triggers a 600% hit on all enemies and a chance to stack Divine Inspiration Canvases. That's up to +3 Chromatic Overflow casts at 6 layers.
- **Against bosses that can't be controlled,** Stain becomes Stain · Flash (−15% damage dealt), so she still weakens the boss.
