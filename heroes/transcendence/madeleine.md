# Star-devouring Bloom - Madeleine

## Summary
A Transcendence Priest built around **Shields**. She trades her own HP for big team shields, and gives heavily shielded allies **Frantic Gluttony** (more Attack and Speed, plus debuff removal). Each time she is healed, she shields 3 more allies, and she can stun the enemies with the lowest HP.

> Game rule: you can't put more than one Transcendence hero with the same name in a lineup.

## Skills
Skill text copied from the game. The tier at which each skill unlocks is in brackets.

### Active: Time to Feast!
Deals (1000% of Attack) damage to 3 enemies with the lowest HP and reduces their Control Immunity by 10% for 2 rounds; meanwhile, restores self HP by 15% of Max. HP. Afterward, grants **Frantic Gluttony** to allies whose Shield value is equal to or higher than 30% of their Max. HP for 2 rounds and removes 1 random debuff from them in the meantime. When allies with **Frantic Gluttony** release basic attacks or active skills, restores self HP equal to 6% of lost HP and increases self Energy by 3.

- **Frantic Gluttony:** Skill effect. Not stackable. Increases the owner's Attack by 10% and Speed by 12. When granting Frantic Gluttony, if the target receiving it gains new extra effects of the buff, Frantic Gluttony's duration will be refreshed. When the following conditions are met, the target receiving the buff will gain extra effects:
  - If the target's Shield value is equal to or higher than 40% of their Max. HP, increases their Damage Reduction Offset by 10% and All Damage Dealt by 6%.
  - If the target's Shield value is equal to or higher than 50% of their Max. HP, besides the effects above, additionally increases their All-damage Reduction Offset by 10%; when the owner releases basic attacks or active skills, additionally inflicts damage by 10% of the target's Max. HP (caps at 3000% of the owner's Attack).

### Passive: Ravenous Hunger (Tier 2 unlock)
Changes the basic attack to: Deals (1000% of Attack) damage to 2 enemies with the lowest HP, reduces their Damage Reduction by 5% for 2 rounds, and restores self HP by 10% of lost HP.

### Passive: Boundless Sepal (Tier 4 unlock)
When Star-devouring Bloom - Madeleine is healed, grants 3 random allies a **Shield** equal to 300% of her Attack. This effect can be triggered up to 3 times in each round and when the limit is reached, there is a 40% chance to inflict **Stun** on each of 3 enemies with the lowest HP for 2 rounds.

- **Shield:** Skill effect. Offsets the Direct Damage and Damage Over Time received. It will disappear when the strength of the shield is 0. The shield strength caps at 100% of self HP.
- **Stun:** Control effect. Stunned heroes are unable to release their active skills, basic attacks, extra attacks, or counterattacks.

### Passive: Infuse with Flavor (Tier 6 unlock)
When self has more than 100 Energy, if self HP is not lower than 70%, deducts self HP equal to 60% of Max. HP, grants self and back-line allies a Shield (cannot be granted repeatedly) equal to 32% of their Max. HP, and increases their Control Immunity by 10% for 2 rounds; if self HP is lower than 70%, restores self HP by 8% of Max. HP and grants self a **Shield** equal to 8% of Max. HP. This effect can only be triggered 1 time in 1 round.

## How she works (analysis, unverified in play)
- **Shields unlock her buffs.** Frantic Gluttony only goes to allies whose shield is at least 30% of their Max HP, with bonuses at 40% and 50%. Her Tier 6 skill (a 32% shield for herself and the back line), Boundless Sepal, and other shield-giving heroes can push allies past those thresholds.
- **She powers herself by healing.** She heals from her active skill, her basic attack (10% of lost HP), and each time an ally with Frantic Gluttony acts (6% of lost HP). Each heal can set off Boundless Sepal, up to 3 shields per round, followed by a 40% chance per target to stun the 3 enemies with the lowest HP.
- **Her HP cost can backfire.** Infuse with Flavor takes 60% of her Max HP. If she's focused right after, that's risky, though her heals and shield help her recover.
- **She sets up control.** Her active skill lowers enemy Control Immunity by 10%, which helps the Boundless Sepal stun and other heroes' control effects land.
