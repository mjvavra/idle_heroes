# Stygian Twilight - Vespera

## Summary
A Transcendence Priest who weakens the enemy's strongest hero with **Lethal Incense** and buffs the whole team. Each round she gives **Stygian Mark** to the ally with the highest Attack, which doubles her active skill's buffs on that ally and gives them bonus hits on enemies with Lethal Incense. **Veil of Mist** cuts the damage enemies deal to her.

> Game rule: you can't put more than one Transcendence hero with the same name in a lineup.

## Skills
Skill text copied from the game. The tier at which each skill unlocks is in brackets.

### Active: Soulcall of Stygian Night
Deals (800% of Attack) damage to all enemies. Afterward, inflicts **Lethal Incense I** on 1 enemy with the highest Attack for 2 rounds; meanwhile: reduces their Attack by 10%, Armor by 10%, Damage Reduction by 10%, Control Immunity by 10%, and Speed by 60 for 2 rounds;
besides:
increases all allies' Attack by 5%, Armor by 5%, Damage Reduction by 5%, Control Immunity by 5%, and Speed by 18 for 2 rounds.
Ally with **Stygian Mark** gains 100% more bonus above.

- **Lethal Incense I:** Skill effect. Not stackable. Reduces the owner's All Damage Dealt by 8%. If the owner has 100 or more Energy when **Lethal Incense** is removed, they lose 40 Energy, and there is a 30% chance to inflict **Lethal Incense** on 1 of their allies without **Lethal Incense** for 2 rounds. If the owner has less than 100 Energy, the owner takes (10% of their Max. HP) damage instead (caps at 1500% of Stygian Twilight - Vespera's Attack).

### Passive: Ferrywoman of Abyss (Tier 2 unlock)
Changes the basic attack to: Deals (400% of Attack) damage to back-line enemies and there is a 50% chance to inflict **Veil of Mist** for 2 rounds. Afterward, increases front-line allies' Damage Reduction by 6% and Control Immunity by 6% for 2 rounds; then increases back-line allies' Attack by 6% for 2 rounds.

- **Veil of Mist:** Skill effect. Reduces 30% All Damage Dealt by the owner to the inflictor. Not stackable.

### Passive: Faint Fragrance (Tier 4 unlock)
At the beginning of each round, grants **Stygian Mark** to the ally with the highest Attack for 1 round. When the ally with **Stygian Mark** releases basic attacks or active skills, additionally attacks the targets with **Lethal Incense**, dealing (6% of their Max. HP) damage (caps at 1500% of the attacker's Attack); meanwhile, restores (10% of the lost HP) HP for the ally with the lowest HP.

- **Stygian Mark:** Skill effect. Not stackable. Increases the owner's All Damage Dealt by 6%. For every 1 time the hero is granted with **Stygian Mark** repeatedly, increases the bonus by 100% and it can be increased up to 2 times.

### Passive: Fatal Allure (Tier 6 unlock)
When self takes damage from basic attacks or active skills, there is a 50% chance to inflict **Veil of Mist** on the attacker for 2 rounds. If the attacker has **Veil of Mist**, there is a 50% chance to inflict **Lethal Incense I** on them for 2 rounds instead.

## How she works (analysis, unverified in play)
- **She shuts down the enemy carry.** Her active skill puts Lethal Incense on the enemy with the highest Attack: −8% damage dealt, plus −10% Attack, Armor, Damage Reduction and Control Immunity, and −60 Speed. When the Incense ends, the target either loses 40 Energy (which delays their active skill) or takes damage based on their Max HP.
- **Stygian Mark makes one ally much stronger.** The ally with the highest Attack gets the Mark each round. If the same ally keeps getting it, the damage bonus grows from 6% to 12% to 18%. The Mark also doubles her active skill's team buffs on that ally, and their attacks hit Incensed enemies for 6% of Max HP while healing your lowest-HP ally.
- **She protects herself.** Veil of Mist from her basic attack and Tier 6 cuts the damage those enemies deal *to her* by 30%. Attackers who already have the Veil can get Lethal Incense instead.
- **Her team buffs are wide:** front-line allies get Damage Reduction and Control Immunity, back-line allies get Attack, and her active skill buffs everyone.
